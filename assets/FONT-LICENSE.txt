Modifications to Tom Thumb for improved readability are by Robey Pointer, see
http://robey.lag.net/2010/01/23/tiny-monospace-font.html — the original author
authorised relicensing of those modifications, and Brian Swetland has since
authorised the font under CC0 or CC-BY 3.0. This port uses it under CC0.

The glyph table in `src/gfx/text.rs` is that font's data, converted from the BDF
to the Adafruit GFX font format (William Skellenger, 2016) and then to Rust. It
is not PICO-8's own font, which lives in PICO-8's ROM and is not part of the cart.
